## What Makes a Good LinkedIn Headshot

A LinkedIn photo has a narrow job: your face fills most of the frame, the background is plain, the lighting is even, and the image still looks like you on a video call. LinkedIn recommends a square image of at least 400 × 400 pixels, according to Aragon AI's pricing-page FAQ — most services deliver far above that. When a result comes back small, a [free AI image upscaler](/posts/best-free-ai-image-upscaler/) can lift it, but upscaling cannot fix a face that does not resemble you.

Likeness is the real quality test, and the hardest to judge from a vendor's own samples. Vizologi ran the same source photos through more than a dozen platforms and weighted resemblance at 70% of the score, because a headshot that does not look like you is useless no matter how sharp it is. Webdesign.org's 12-platform comparison in 2026 agreed: expect to discard a meaningful share of any batch, and judge a service by how many usable keepers its batch size and refund policy give you.

## Aragon AI: Best Overall

Aragon AI sells one-time packs with no subscription: Basic delivers 40 headshots in about 45 minutes, Standard 60, and Executive 100 in about 15 minutes, with prices across the packs in the $35–$75 range, per its pricing page and the ai-alternative.com review checked against vendor pages in October 2026. Basic and Standard images arrive at 896 × 1088 pixels; Executive upgrades to 1792 × 2176, which matters only for print. Six selfies are enough to start.

Two things set Aragon apart. First, it has a limited free generator — ai-alternative.com calls it the only free generator in its roundup worth trying first. Second, its privacy posture is the strongest of the group: Aragon states it is SOC 2 Type II compliant, and its pricing page says the generated photos are yours to use however you like, including print and professional profiles. A free redo is available, and refunds apply when you have not downloaded the photos, within 14 days, according to ai-alternative.com.

## HeadshotPro: Best for Teams

HeadshotPro is the fastest to set up, needing only one to three selfies. Its three one-time packages sit in the $29–$59 range: Basic gives 30 headshots in about two hours, Professional 50 in about 30 minutes, and Executive 70 in about 15 minutes with 4K downloads, according to its pricing page. Every package includes 1 free redo within 60 days. There is no subscription and team credits never expire, which suits companies that onboard people over time.

Its privacy page is unusually explicit: uploaded selfies and generated results auto-delete after 30 days, download links last seven days, and the site states plainly that photos are never used to train AI models. Its SOC 2 Type II report is available to every customer, and every package carries a 30-day money-back Realism Guarantee — if the results do not look like you, you get a redo or a refund. Team discounts start at 20% off and climb toward 50% off.

## Secta Labs: Best for Volume

Secta Labs is the volume play: a one-time fee under $50 buys a large batch across 90+ styles, with a 30-day money-back guarantee. The exact count is the one figure vendors and reviewers disagree on: Secta's own pricing card says 100+ HD photos, its marketing pages say 200+ and even 300+, and reviewers who counted report batches in the 100–300 range. Treat the count as "100-plus, often several hundred."

There is no free tier — you pay through Stripe first, then upload. The service asks for 25 or more source photos — some of its help pages have shown 20, so go by the number on the upload page — and its help pages warn against uploading only selfies. On privacy, Secta states SOC 2 compliance, and saastrials.com, which checked 17 generators by hand in November 2026, reports that your data is deleted within 14 days of a confirmed request.

## StudioShot: Best for Included Retouches

StudioShot is still operating and selling per-photoshoot packages, with prices across its three packages in the $29–$44 range, read from its own site on November 9, 2026 by novaheadshot.com. Essential gives 40 images in one style, Pro 80 in two, and Signature 120 in three. Turnaround runs under three hours on Essential, under two on Pro, and under one on Signature, with free retouches included — up to two, four, and six respectively. Team plans and a 100% money-back guarantee are listed on its pricing page.

StudioShot's differentiator is the human touch: its styles are designed by credited photographers, and the retouch allowance means a real person can fix the small things the model gets wrong, such as a stray hair or an odd collar. The trade-off is fewer styles per package than Secta or Aragon, so it suits a buyer who wants one polished corporate look.

## PortraitPal: Best Simple One-Off

PortraitPal keeps the decision simple: three one-time packages in the $35–$75 range — Quick Refresh with 20 headshots, Career Upgrade with 60, and Executive Pack with 100 — with no subscription anywhere, according to blogiantic's capture of its pricing page on November 6, 2026. Its privacy statement is the clearest in the group after HeadshotPro's: PortraitPal says it does not train AI on your photos, never sells your data, deletes training images after a month, and lets you request deletion or a full account purge at any time, as summarized by aipure.ai and blogiantic. Generated images come with full usage rights for personal and professional use. There is no free plan, and a money-back or satisfaction guarantee is listed.

## How to Get a Usable Result

The upload matters more than the vendor. Every service's help pages give the same advice: use varied, well-lit photos taken at different times, mix angles and expressions, and skip sunglasses, heavy filters, and group shots. Secta asks for at least 25 images and warns against uploading only selfies; HeadshotPro needs only one to three.

Once the batch arrives, judge it like a recruiter would. Open the top candidates at the size they will actually appear — a small circle on a phone screen — and discard anything where the eyes, smile, or jawline look off. Keep two or three finalists, check them against a real photo of yourself, and only then download. Remember the refund clock: Aragon's refund applies before you download, and both HeadshotPro's and Secta's guarantees run 30 days.

## FAQ

### Is an AI headshot acceptable on LinkedIn?

Yes. LinkedIn has no rule against AI-generated profile photos, and recruiters care whether the photo looks like you, not how it was made.

### How many selfies do I need to upload?

It depends on the service: HeadshotPro needs one to three, Aragon AI asks for six, PortraitPal five to ten, and Secta Labs 25 or more.

### Are AI headshots free anywhere?

A genuinely free dedicated service is rare. The free routes are general-purpose tools — the Gemini app, Canva, and ChatGPT — plus limited free previews from Aragon AI and Try It On AI. Expect to pay in the $29–$75 range.

### Will the service use my photos to train its AI?

It depends on the vendor. HeadshotPro and PortraitPal state explicitly that they never train on your photos.

### Can I use an AI headshot commercially?

Most vendors grant full personal and professional use, and Aragon AI states the photos are yours to use including print. For Secta Labs, confirm commercial use in writing first, as its licence terms were not stated clearly in the pages reviewed here.
